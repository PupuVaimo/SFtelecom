The technology, explained

How do we bring internet where mobile coverage is poor?

We create a separate local wireless network. It is not simply another SIM card using the same mobile tower.

🌐

1. Internet source

We obtain a high-capacity internet connection from a fiber network or suitable satellite backhaul.

📡

2. Our network hub

We connect our local site using fiber or a long-distance point-to-point radio link.

🗼

3. SFtelecom base station

Our sector antennas transmit broadband wirelessly to nearby properties.

🏠

4. Your home

A small outdoor receiver connects to your indoor Wi-Fi router. No mobile SIM needed.

Important: fixed wireless still requires suitable radio paths, permitted spectrum, power and a viable backhaul connection. Hills, dense forests and distance may require additional relay sites or another access technology. We confirm feasibility before promising service.

Common questions

What you should know.

Is SFtelecom a real internet provider or just an equipment installer?

The proposed model is an infrastructure-based ISP: SFtelecom would contract with subscribers, operate its own local access network, manage service and support, and obtain upstream internet capacity from a wholesale provider. This site is a project concept, not an active commercial offering.

Does it work where DNA, Elisa and Telia have no signal?

Potentially, yes. Our proposed fixed wireless access network uses its own base stations and outdoor receivers rather than the mobile operators' coverage. However, it still requires a viable connection to the wider internet and a suitable radio path to each property.

Do I need a SIM card?

Not for the main fixed wireless product. A dedicated outdoor receiver communicates with an SFtelecom base station and connects by cable to your Wi-Fi router.

Can you connect a single isolated cottage?

We can assess it, but building a new base station for one cottage may be uneconomical. A nearby existing SFtelecom site, a relay, or an alternative satellite solution could be more practical.

How much does a whole village network cost?

It depends on terrain, distance to an upstream connection, tower access, required capacity, number of properties and installation complexity. A survey and technical design are needed before a responsible quote can be given.

Can seasonal cottages pause their subscriptions?

Flexible seasonal service is part of the planned product concept. Exact pause terms, reconnection fees and any minimum commitments would be specified before launch.
